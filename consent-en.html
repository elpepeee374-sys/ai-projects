Consent to Personal Data Processing

The User, by leaving a request, submitting an inquiry, or completing the interactive survey (quiz) on the website CyprusElite, agrees to accept this Consent to Personal Data Processing (hereinafter – Consent).

By submitting any feedback form on the Website, the User accepts this Consent. Acting freely, of their own free will, and in their own interest, the User gives their consent to the website owners of CyprusElite (hereinafter – Operator) to process their personal data under the following conditions:

1. General Conditions

1.1. This Consent is given for the processing of personal data, both with and without the use of automation tools.

1.2. Consent is given for the processing of the following personal data of the User, which are not special or biometric:

  • Name;
  • Contact phone numbers;
  • Email addresses (if provided);
  • User data (location details; OS type and version; Browser type and version; device type and screen resolution; the source from which the user arrived at the site; language of the OS and Browser; pages opened and buttons clicked; IP address).

2. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

2.1. The purpose of processing personal data is to provide the User with high-quality consulting services for property selection in Cyprus, calculation of return on investment (ROI), contacting the User to clarify the parameters of the request, as well as analytics of the physical person's actions on the website and the website's functioning.

3. Legal Basis and Order of Processing

3.1. The basis for processing personal data is this Consent to Personal Data Processing, and international data protection standards (including the General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR).

3.2. During personal data processing, the following actions may be performed: collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), extraction, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, erasure, destruction.

3.3. Processing of personal data ceases upon achieving the purposes of processing or in the case of revocation of consent by the User.

3.4. Consent may be revoked by the User or their representative by sending a written application to the Operator at the email address: info@cypruselite.com.
